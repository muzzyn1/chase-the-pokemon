HOW IT WORKS
🎯 THE GOAL — Friday, 5AM–3PM in 5 real minutes. 23 other hunters racing you. The winner is whoever has the most CASH at 3PM. Not stock. Cash.
💷 COP → SELL → CASH — Copping an item gives you STOCK, not money. To turn it into cash you go to the CASHOUT shop (gold building near The Ends) or catch a WHATNOT STREAMER who pops up mid-game — pays 30% more but only serves the first 3 hunters to reach him. Unsold stock at 3PM gets dumped for half price.
📈 THE MARKET — Prices move all day. Tap 📱 → MARKET tab. One Piece might be pumping while Prismatic's crashing. Sell into pumps, hold through dips. Or don't. That's the game.
🕹 CONTROLS — WASD/arrows (joystick on phone). Walk up to a shop's front door to interact. scroll or +/− to zoom — zoom right out for the full map.
📱 PINGS — Drop intel hits your phone. ACCURATE = trust it. VAGUE = could be gold, could be a dud. SPECIAL DROPS (purple) = tiny stock, silly margins, everyone sprints. Tap SET ROUTE and follow the arrow.
🧍 QUEUES + CARRY — 1 per customer, but LOOP the queue if stock's still there. You can only carry so much — when your pockets are full you MUST go sell before copping more.
🏪 BOSSMAN SHOP — Dodgy corner shop. Cheap pings, decent stock, but sometimes it's COUNTERFEIT. If you get done, either punch Bossman up for your money back or bin the fakes.
❤️ HEALTH & STAMINA — Moving drains stamina, standing still recovers it. Scraps cost health. Hit zero on either = lose 30 game-minutes. Your character's quirks WILL activate.
🗺 THE TOWN — You start in THE ESTATE (south-west). The HIGH STREET runs east-west through the middle — Argos, Tesco, Sainsbury's, John Lewis, LGS, Bossman, CASHOUT all sit on it. The RETAIL PARK (Smyths, B&M, Sports Direct, Costco) is south-east down the big road. ASDA's up north by the canal. Learn the routes — the map view (zoom out) helps.
