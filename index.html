HOW IT WORKS
🎯 THE GOAL — It's Friday, 5AM–3PM compressed into 5 real minutes. 23 other hunters are racing you. Biggest haul value (£) at 3PM wins the crown.
🕹 CONTROLS — WASD/arrows to move (joystick on phone). Walk up to a store's front door to interact. scroll or the +/− buttons zoom the camera — zoom right out for the full map.
📱 PINGS — Your phone buzzes with drop intel. ACCURATE pings = trust them. VAGUE pings = could be gold, could be a dud, could be the wrong store. Tap SET ROUTE and the arrow guides you.
🧍 QUEUES — Every store is 1 per customer, but you can LOOP the queue if stock's still there (security might clock you). Big stores = big queues but fat stock. Watch the live feed — when it says a store's nearly done, it's nearly done.
❤️ HEALTH & STAMINA — Moving drains stamina, standing still recovers it. Scraps and obstacles cost health. Hit zero on either and you lose 30 game-minutes. Your character's quirks WILL activate. Deal with it.
